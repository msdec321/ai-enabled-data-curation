# AutoDQA — Autonomous Data Quality Assessment

This is a multi-agent system for autonomous data quality profiling, issue detection, and root cause investigation against a clinical data warehouse (CDW).

If you are the **coordinator agent**: read `COORDINATOR.md` for your instructions.
If you are a **profiler agent**: read `PROFILER.md` for your instructions.
If you are an **analyst agent**: read `ANALYST.md` for your instructions.
If you are an **investigator agent**: read `INVESTIGATOR.md` for your instructions.
If you are a **reviewer agent**: read `REVIEW.md` for your instructions.
If you are a **report-writing agent**: read `REPORT_WRITER.md` for your instructions.

Your role will be specified in the prompt you received.
